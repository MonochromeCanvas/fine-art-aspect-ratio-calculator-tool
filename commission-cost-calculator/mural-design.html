That’s a good reason to get in touch. Tell Joëlle what matters most. A simpler approach, fewer pieces or a different format may be a better starting point. There’s no need to make every decision here.

How are the starting prices put together?

The ranges reflect Monochrome Canvas’s starting scopes, not a universal rate card. Your final quote depends on the actual work and how you plan to use it. Choosing a deadline or describing commercial use here never stacks hidden percentage fees.

The Graphic Artists Guild Handbook is one useful starting point for thinking about creative pricing. Its guidance makes a useful point: “no two jobs are exactly alike.” The work, intended use, budget and timing all matter. Joëlle considers these alongside the time, skill, materials and detail involved in each commission—whether it’s a painting, illustration, mural design or graphic design. Read the publisher’s excerpt from the Handbook, 16th edition, or explore the current 17th edition. These references offer a framework for the conversation. The starting ranges here reflect Joëlle’s own work and project scope, rather than a price list taken from the book.
